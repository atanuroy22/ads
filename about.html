About DevTools

Welcome to DevTools, your go-to source for simple, fast, and secure developer utilities and online tools.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide web developers, content creators, and students with reliable, easy-to-use tools that operate directly within the browser. We believe in speed and privacy, which is why our core tools process data locally on your device without sending any of your sensitive text to our servers.

What We Offer

At DevTools, we feature a growing collection of free, browser-based utilities:

  • Base64 Encoder: Encode text to Base64 with single and double encoding options.
  • Word Counter: Real-time text analysis with word, character, and paragraph counts.
  • Password Generator: Create strong, secure passwords with customizable settings.
  • JSON Formatter: Format, validate, and minify JSON data instantly.
  • URL Encoder: Encode and decode URLs, query parameters, and URI components.
  • Case Converter: Convert text between uppercase, lowercase, title case, camelCase, and more.
  • Lorem Ipsum Generator: Generate placeholder text for designs and mockups.
  • Color Converter: Pick colors and convert between HEX, RGB, and HSL formats.
  • Hash Generator: Generate SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, and SHA-512 hashes.
  • HTML Encoder: Encode or decode HTML entities for safe web display.

We are continuously working to expand our suite of tools based on user feedback and industry needs.

Who We Are

DevTools is maintained by a small team of passionate developers dedicated to creating high-quality, free resources for the tech community. We understand the value of quick, accessible utilities in a developer's workflow and strive to keep our platform streamlined and ad-supported to remain free for everyone.
